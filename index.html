Junior Research Fellow and PhD Candidate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

About

I develop reinforcement-learning methods and research software for accelerator beamline control. My doctoral work covers simulation environments, continuous-control training, and comparison with classical optimization methods.

I work across experimental research and production-oriented machine learning, from model development and evaluation to deployment and monitoring. My broader work includes generative models, agentic systems, natural language processing, and computer vision.
